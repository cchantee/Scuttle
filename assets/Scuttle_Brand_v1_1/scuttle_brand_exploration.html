Concept only · No repository changes
01 / The identity

Meet the Scuttle mark.

scuttle.

The proposed symbol is a small, forward-moving creature: a playful abstract mascot rather than a financial institution’s crest. Its curved shell and short legs suggest movement in manageable steps. The wordmark is lowercase and confident.

Brand promise: Make each paycheck feel more manageable.
Voice: Plainspoken, encouraging, never judgmental.
Tagline: Money moves. You’ve got this.

02 / Visual system

Grounded with a spark.

Deep evergreen communicates stability; citrus-lime adds optimism and personality; cream keeps dense financial screens readable; coral is reserved for caution and debt.
