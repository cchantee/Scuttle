# Coastal artwork provenance

The human supplied the palette, shoreline/crab reference, requested a simplified
watercolor treatment, and required matching the existing in-app doodle crab.
Artwork was generated with the built-in imagegen tool under that direction.
This is AI-generated artwork, not a human-painted asset; distinguish it from the
human-authored design decisions in study documentation.

Final project asset: `assets/coastal/shoreline-watercolor-v2.png`.
Original line-drawn crab remains code-native in `src/components/coastal.tsx`.
No font outlines or source font bytes were modified.

## Generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: a transparent decorative illustration
for Scuttle, a calm personal budgeting app. User-directed design: create a
SIMPLIFIED version of the watercolor coastal graphic in their reference: a low
shoreline of pale sand, two soft blue/teal wave washes, a small friendly coral crab
with two claws and simple dark eyes, a few sea-grass blades and two rounded rocks.
Minimal hand-painted watercolor/doodle hybrid, restrained soft edges and light
paper-pigment texture, broad simple shapes rather than detailed scenery. The scene
is a wide low horizontal strip, approximately 3:1, all illustration clustered in
the bottom portion, crab right of center and sea grass at far right, ample
transparent space above, no text, no lettering, no interface, no device mockup,
no shadowed frame. Exact palette: Coastal Blue #8FB3C0, Sea Glass #DCE7DA, Soft
Coral #D58A71, Warm Sand #E4B793, Seashell #EFD3C1, Morning Mist #D9DFE6, Muted
Teal #869F9F. Very sparse dark #344447 line details on crab only. True transparent
background, not a cream rectangular backdrop. Keep whole scene visible inside canvas.

## Final edit prompt

Edit target: the supplied transparent coastal shoreline illustration. Change ONLY
the crab character to match this exact existing Scuttle app doodle: a small broad
horizontal oval body, muted coral #D58A71 watercolor fill, sparse thin dark charcoal
#344447 outlines, two short vertical eye stalks on top ending in tiny dark circular
eyes, a tiny curved smiling mouth in the middle, two skinny outward raised arms
leading to small simple crescent/open-pincer claws with pale seashell #EFD3C1 fill,
three skinny angled line legs on each side. Cute minimal hand-drawn doodle rather
than a fleshy rounded cartoon. Keep the crab at its existing position and scale.
Preserve the sand, two blue/teal wave washes, sea grass, rocks, restrained palette,
low wide composition, watercolor texture, and real transparency exactly. No text,
no new objects, no frame. Preserve all pixels other than the crab and its immediate
boundary as closely as possible.
